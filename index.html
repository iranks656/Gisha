Founded in 2018 at Cyanika, on the Uganda–Rwanda border, Gisha Freighters brings logistics, clearing and tax education together under one roof.

⌖CyanikaUganda · Rwanda border
Established2018

Gisha Freighters Uganda Ltd is a licensed clearing and forwarding company in Uganda. We provide efficient import and export solutions, customs clearance and professional tax advisory support.

Known also as Gisha Logistics & Tax Services, we take a customer-first approach: helping goods move across borders while empowering businesses with tax knowledge and compliance support.

Our wider services also include tax advisory and education, warehousing and distribution solutions, and business compliance and consultancy.

01 / OUR VISION

To be the leading logistics and tax education partner in East Africa, recognized for excellence, innovation, and trust in every service we deliver.

02 / OUR MISSION

To simplify cross-border trade and tax compliance by providing reliable, affordable, and transparent logistics, clearing, and educational services that empower businesses and individuals to succeed.
