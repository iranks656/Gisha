WHO WE ARE

A dependable partner
for cross-border trade.

Gisha Freighters Uganda Ltd is a licensed clearing and forwarding company in Uganda. The company was founded in 2018 on the border of Uganda and Rwanda at Cyanika.

Gisha Logistics & Tax Services is a modern logistics, clearing and forwarding, and tax education company based in Uganda. We specialize in efficient import and export solutions, customs clearance, and professional tax advisory services.

With a customer-first approach, we help goods move smoothly across borders while empowering businesses with tax knowledge and compliance support.

Our Vision

To be the leading logistics and tax education partner in East Africa, recognized for excellence, innovation and trust.

Our Mission

To simplify cross-border trade and tax compliance through reliable, affordable and transparent services.
