# Optional service ideas for Gisha Freighters

These are recommendations for discussion, not claims that Gisha currently provides them. Confirm operating capability, partner agreements, prices, routes and any licensing obligations before adding any item to the public service list.

## Strongest near-term additions

1. **Shipment milestone updates.** Give customers clear status updates at booking, document readiness, border/port arrival, customs release and delivery. Start with a dependable WhatsApp or email update process; only advertise live tracking if a carrier or system actually supplies it.
2. **Consolidated freight for smaller shipments.** Consider less-than-container-load (LCL) or groupage options through vetted freight partners, with published cut-off dates, routes, minimums and transparent handling fees.
3. **Pre-shipment document review.** Offer a structured checklist review before cargo departs, tailored by route and commodity. Keep it clear that final import requirements and assessments come from URA and other competent authorities.

## Add only with a qualified partner or confirmed capability

- **Cargo insurance coordination:** arrange cover through an authorised insurer or broker and explain exclusions, limits and claims steps. Do not present Gisha as an insurer unless appropriately authorised.
- **Door-to-door delivery:** define the origin/destination areas, hand-off responsibility, delivery proof and exceptions before marketing it.
- **Vehicle sourcing/import support:** this is a possible separate vertical, but it needs verified sourcing partners, inspection practices, current URA valuation knowledge and clear responsibility boundaries. The existing business information does not confirm vehicle sourcing or a dedicated duty calculator.

The safest website change is to publish only services Gisha can currently deliver or contractually arrange. When these ideas are approved, add the confirmed scope, coverage, pricing basis and exclusions to the service page and matching FAQs.
